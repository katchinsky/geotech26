How the deck works

Your first pass shows every card once, in random order. After that, review has three levels. A card is Missed after a wrong answer, Learning after one correct answer, and Solid after two correct answers in a row. About 85% of review cards come from Missed and Learning, with Missed cards three times as likely as Learning ones. The other 15% are Solid cards, and each time the one you have not seen for the longest comes up, so the same ones do not keep repeating. A wrong answer sends a Solid card straight back to Missed. Nothing ever leaves the deck. The same card is never shown twice within a few draws.

Keys: T for true, F for false, Enter for the next card. Progress is saved in this browser.
